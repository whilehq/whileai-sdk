A stock LangChain Deep Agents agent on Qwen3.8-27B reviews real patches and says approve or
reject. Its traces go to LangSmith. We fine-tune it twice on those traces with smithtune's own
renderer, split and schedule: once on the traces smithtune's judge council keeps, once on the
traces `wai.simulations.optimize` keeps. Nothing else differs. Both models are scored on SWE-bench Verified
patch review, a benchmark nobody here built, and on 84 repositories the traces never touched.

What you will learn: that which traces you train on moves a fine-tune as much as the trainer
does, how to hold a comparison to one variable (same trainer, same server, same sampling), and
why a single base run is not a baseline. You need `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` and `LANGSMITH_API_KEY`
for traces, Docker or Modal for smithtune (it imports `fcntl`, so Linux or macOS; `smithtune_modal.py`
runs it on Modal), a Baseten key for its default council, and a Modal account for
training and serving. `python run.py --dry-run` needs none of it.

## Result

**The fair rerun** (`PREREGISTRATION.md`, merged before training). The smithtune side follows
smithtune's own skill: read 20 traces, co-write the rubric (`rubric_codesigned.md`), check it on
a 20-trace trial (`calibration/`), then run the council. Both sides are cut to the same size,
fine-tuned with smithtune's renderer, split and schedule for exactly 2 epochs, three seeds each,
and scored on one server. Each arm pools its three seeds; comparisons are paired by review.

**Experiment A: same 262 traces, two ways to pick 174.**

| | smithtune skill path | While's pick | While minus smithtune |
|---|---|---|---|
| SWE-bench Verified (250 reviews) | 35.6% (seeds 40.0, 24.4, 42.4) | **52.0%** (53.6, 50.4, 52.0) | **+16.4 [+12.5, +20.3]** |
| Held-out repos (246 reviews) | 58.1% (59.8, 49.6, 65.0) | **67.9%** (69.1, 68.7, 65.9) | **+9.8 [+6.5, +13.1]** |
| Model calls per review, SWE-bench Verified | 27.7 | **20.1** | **-7.6 [-8.6, -6.7]** |

Every While seed beats every smithtune seed on both benchmarks (9 of 9 pairs). The two picks share
142 of their 174 traces: the co-designed rubric kept 184 of 260 where the first rubric kept 258,
so reading traces with an agent moves smithtune most of the way to While's pick. The 32 traces
that differ are the long correct reviews the council still keeps, and a model trained on them runs
out of steps without a verdict on 96 to 165 of 250 reviews, against 45 to 65 for While's.

**Experiment B: 400 more rollouts per side.** smithtune's side drew 400 unused tasks at random
(it cannot aim); While's drew 400 aimed at the stock agent's weak spots (`aim.py`). Each side added
its rollouts to the original pool and picked with its own method; both cut to 317 traces.
While's side is ahead by **+17.3 [+13.9, +20.9]** on SWE-bench Verified (47.5% against 30.1%) and
**+13.7 [+10.3, +17.4]** on the held-out repos, 8 of 9 seed pairs. Neither side beats its own
Experiment A version, so the extra 400 rollouts did not help either side; B separates the
pickers, not the value of aiming.

**The registered schedule.** As first registered, training stopped when validation loss stopped
improving (smithtune's default). It kept epoch 1 on 10 of 12 runs, on losses about 0.001 apart
from epoch 2, and epoch-1 checkpoints mostly run out of steps. Experiment A under that schedule:
+9.5 [+6.4, +12.8] for While's pick, with seed accuracies from 22% to 59% inside one arm. It is
published as registered (`rows/srv-a-*-s*`); amendment 1 moved both experiments to 2 epochs
before Experiment B was scored.

All numbers are in `results.json` under `rerun` (`python make_results.py`).

**The first result** (2026-09-28, one seed per arm, smithtune rubric written without reading
traces, council kept 258): SWE-bench Verified 46.0% against **56.8%**, +10.8 [+4.8, +16.8], with 5.3
fewer calls; held-out repos a tie (63.8% against 61.4%). Base model, three runs: 11.6%, 12.0%, 9.6%.

The write-up with charts: [Deep Agents SFT from traces](https://while.ai/blog/deepagents-sft-from-traces).

## How it works

1. `data.py` builds the review tasks from `nebius/SWE-agent-trajectories` and
   `nebius/SWE-bench-extra` (pinned revisions): for each issue with a passing and a failing
   SWE-agent patch, one of each. Splits are by repository. `wai.decontaminate` checks train and dev
   against the holdout (0 of 1,306 dropped, every rule ran). Four snapshots deleted from GitHub are
   listed in `unavailable.json` and left out everywhere.
2. `collect.py` runs the stock agent (`agent.py`: `create_deep_agent`, read-only filesystem over a
   tarball of the base commit) and writes LangSmith feedback `correctness` and `model_calls` on every
   root run. 400 training reviews: 67% correct.
3. Without While: `smithtune dataset pull` with the correctness filter, then `smithtune dataset
   triage` with `rubric.md`. The council kept 258 of 260.
4. With While: `pick.py` runs `wai.simulations.optimize(mode="sft")` with reward 1 only for a correct review
   within 8 model calls, and writes feedback `while_pick = 1`; smithtune pulls it with that filter.
   173 kept.
5. `export_sft.py` runs inside the smithtune image and calls smithtune's `prepare_sft_rows`
   (reasoning omitted, its default), `split_rows` (80/10/10) and `render_row_tokens` (the Baseten
   renderer: one datum per assistant turn, history at weight 0). `smithtune prepare` itself stops
   without a Baseten Loops or Fireworks account; this skips only that preflight.
6. `train_modal.py` trains LoRA on one H200 with smithtune's Baseten defaults: rank 8, batch 32,
   learning rate 1e-4, seed 42, up to 5 epochs, stop when validation loss does not improve, keep the
   best epoch. Both arms stopped after epoch 3 and kept epoch 2.
7. `serve_modal.py` serves the base and both adapters from one vLLM 0.26 server with the token-level
   Qwen3.5 tool parser (`qwen35_tool_parser.py`) and `enable_thinking=false` for every request.
8. `public_bench.py` builds the SWE-bench Verified set from six public leaderboard submissions
   (`s3://swe-bench-submissions`, `patch.diff` and `report.json`), none of whose twelve repositories
   is in the training traces. `report.py` and `make_results.py` do the paired comparisons.

## Run it

```bash
cd recipes/community/deepagents-review-four-arms
python run.py --dry-run                    # offline: the comparison and the pick rule on fixture rows
python data.py && python public_bench.py   # tasks (byte-identical from the pinned revisions)
python run.py traces                       # stock agent over train -> LangSmith
python pick.py                             # While's pick -> feedback while_pick=1
docker build -f smithtune.Dockerfile -t smithtune:0.1.0 .
# smithtune dataset pull / triage / push, then export_sft.py in the image (see step 3-5)
modal deploy train_modal.py                # then spawn train for sft-with and sft-without
REVIEW_ADAPTERS=sft-with,sft-without modal deploy serve_modal.py
python collect.py --arm srv-sft-with --split public --base-url <server>/v1 --model sft-with --api-key-env REVIEW_SERVER_KEY
python make_results.py
```

### Run the fair rerun

Every step of `PREREGISTRATION.md`, in order. `smithtune_modal.py` runs the smithtune CLI in the
pinned image on Modal; Docker works the same way with `smithtune.Dockerfile`.

```bash
ST_PY=/root/.local/share/uv/tools/smithtune/bin/python   # the Python smithtune is installed in

# Experiment A: the smithtune skill path, then the cut, then 2-epoch training
modal run smithtune_modal.py --cmd "smithtune dataset pull trial-v2 <source> --target-count 20 --max-candidates 20"
modal run smithtune_modal.py --cmd "smithtune dataset triage trial-v2 --rubric /recipe/rubric_codesigned.md --confirm"
modal run smithtune_modal.py --cmd "smithtune dataset pull council-v2 <source> --target-count 400 --max-candidates 1000"
modal run smithtune_modal.py --cmd "smithtune dataset triage council-v2 --rubric /recipe/rubric_codesigned.md --confirm"
python equalize.py a                       # both sides cut to the same size -> keep/
modal run smithtune_modal.py --cmd "$ST_PY /recipe/export_sft.py council-v2 a-without --keep keep/a-without.json"
modal run smithtune_modal.py --cmd "$ST_PY /recipe/export_sft.py while a-with --keep keep/a-with.json"
modal run --detach train_modal.py --arm a-with --seed 42 --epochs 2      # and 43, 44, and a-without

# Experiment B: 400 aimed and 400 random new rollouts, each side picks its own way
python aim.py                              # writes aim.json before any rollout
python collect.py --arm b-random --split train --tasks aim.json:random   # LANGSMITH_PROJECT=deepagents-review-b-random
python collect.py --arm b-aimed  --split train --tasks aim.json:aimed    # LANGSMITH_PROJECT=deepagents-review-b-aimed
python pick.py --arms base,b-aimed --key while_pick_b --out while_pick_b.json --target 1000
# smithtune: pull council-b-random and triage it with the same rubric; pull while-b-aimed
python equalize.py b                       # then export_sft.py --also, and train as in A

# Score all twelve adapters on one server, then the numbers
REVIEW_ADAPTERS=a-with-e2-s42,...,b-without-e2-s44 modal deploy serve_modal.py
python collect.py --arm srv-a-with-e2-s42 --split public --base-url <server>/v1 --model a-with-e2-s42 --api-key-env REVIEW_SERVER_KEY
# ...the same for each adapter, and again with --split holdout
python make_results.py
```

`<source>` is the LangSmith workspace, project, window and correctness filter, written out in
`PREREGISTRATION.md`.

## Caveats

- Three seeds per arm. Seed spread inside one arm is up to 18 points even at a fixed 2 epochs
  (smithtune skill path on SWE-bench Verified: 24.4% to 42.4%), so the paired intervals, which
  bootstrap over reviews, understate the full uncertainty. The seed-pair counts (9 of 9 in A) are
  the check that does not lean on them.
- The schedule changed after Experiment A was scored (amendment 1, early stopping to 2 epochs).
  The registered result is published beside it and points the same way, +9.5 [+6.4, +12.8].
- smithtune's converter dropped one While-pick trace in each experiment for a structural problem,
  so While's side trained on one fewer trace (173 against 174, 316 against 317).
- smithtune omits reasoning from training rows by default, so every tuned model is non-thinking
  and is scored with thinking off. With thinking on, through OpenRouter, the base scored 70.3%,
  75.1% and 74.6% on the held-out repositories.
- Training ran on Modal, not Baseten Loops (not enabled for our workspace), with smithtune's
  renderer, split and schedule. The council ran on Baseten, smithtune's default.
- An agent read the 20 traces and drafted `rubric_codesigned.md`; a person approved it from a
  summary. The LangChain team is invited to send a rubric of their own, and we will run it the
  same way.
